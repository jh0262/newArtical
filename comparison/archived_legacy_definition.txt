P0 definition
P0 and Full use the same normalized decision coordinates, constraints, fixed objective transforms, population and archive sizes of 100, and 250 generations. For P0, the archive covariance is symmetrized and its nonnegative eigenvalues λⱼ are sorted in descending order with eigenvectors V. If fewer than two archive points exist, the covariance is zero. Writing λ̄ for the mean of the D eigenvalues and setting ρ=10⁻⁶, its spectral preprocessing is
rj=(1−ρ)λj+ρ max(λ,10−12),    r=D−1j=1Drj
hj=max(rj,10−16)max(r,10−16),    qj=min{2,max{0.25,hj}},    sj=qjD−1k=1Dqk2
The clip applies before the final unit-mean-square renormalization. Therefore the final sⱼ values need not lie in [0.25,2]. At generation g=1,…,G, P0 samples each guide aᵢ uniformly from the entire archive and forms
σg=0.15−0.14g−1max(G−1,1)
ui=yi+0.5(ai−yi)+σgV diag(s)zi+0.05IgPz′i
Here zᵢ and z′ᵢ are independent standard Gaussian vectors. P is the orthogonal projector onto eigenvectors satisfying λⱼ≤0.1 max(λ̄,10⁻¹²). Ig=1 when the incoming HV stagnation counter is at least five and this subspace is nonempty. The fixed 0.05 pulse is then applied to every offspring. Whenever the counter is at least five, P0 resets it even if the subspace is empty. After archive updating, an absolute normalized HV change below 10⁻⁴ increments the counter; otherwise it resets to zero. P0 has no strict-improvement reset or adaptive σ feedback.
P0 clips normalized coordinates to [0,1], applies the common task-specific feasibility repair, evaluates 100 offspring, and performs constraint-prioritized nondominated/crowding environmental selection. It uses no differential term, coordinate crossover, or polynomial mutation. The old archive and offspring are filtered by constraint dominance and deduplicated by objective vector in stable order. For two objectives, P0 computes triangle areas once, gives endpoints infinite score, and keeps the 100 largest scores; equal scores follow sorted first-objective order. Three-objective overflow uses crowding distance. Its output is the final feasible archive. Thus P0→Full changes guidance, spectral scaling, differential and mutation operations, feedback, pulse scope, and two-objective retention together.
The archived PACPOSearch.solve legacy branch is the authoritative executable P0 definition. Eight first-seed benchmark tapes were also reproduced exactly by the P5Core legacy branch with one computational thread, matching decisions, histories, and archives. Replay reads saved objectives and adds no independent samples. The joint control supplies the incremental comparison with Full’s guided differential update.
